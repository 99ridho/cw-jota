5. MEDIUM, R-04, FIXED. Sparkles (decode button, All Seven badge), Gem (Perfect Round), and Star (score) were generic glyphs. They are now Eye (reveal the message), ListChecks (all letters learned), Target (no misses), and Trophy (score).

7. MEDIUM, R-08, KEPT WITH REASON. Arrows appear only on buttons that move to the next screen, so the arrow always means "forward".
8. LOW, R-29, KEPT AS OWNER DIRECTION. The palette has five fills. Three carry meaning (orange for actions, green for correct, yellow for the signal lamp); blue and pink only tint letter cards, stickers, and badges. The owner asked for playful coloring, so this stays.
9. LOW, copy, FIXED. "Learn 7 secret signals" inflated what Morse is; it now says "Learn 7 Morse letters". 10. LOW, copy, FIXED. The station invitation now reads "Come and try a real radio at the station." in place of the exclamation version, and both languages are editable from `.env`.
11. LOW, C-2, FIXED. Six i18n strings were never shown (`app.title`, `hook.listen`, `learn.send.title`, `challenge.submit`, `common.correct`, `common.wrong`); they are removed from both languages.
12. LOW, antislop-code, FIXED. One comment stated a wrong fact (the silent WAV is a few samples, not 0.1 s). Three JSDoc blocks ran to three or four lines for a one-line fact and are cut to one. Two comments restated the code ("Card with hard shadow", "Bordered block with hard shadow") and are removed.
